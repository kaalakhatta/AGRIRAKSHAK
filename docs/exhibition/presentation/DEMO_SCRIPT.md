# Five-Minute Demonstration Script

## 0:00 - 0:45: Home and Introduction
"Welcome. This is the Sehore farm companion exhibition build, configured for soybean, wheat, and gram. All records shown are synthetic fictional samples running locally on this device, with local records and explicit consent for any external lookup or screening request."

## 0:45 - 1:45: Plan
"Here in the Plan tab, we select our Sehore sample plot and view the current crop cycle. As you can see, we have farmer-authored reminders and date/stage options. Please note that reviewed seed guidance is currently unavailable until a genuine agronomy review exists. These are synthetic records."

## 1:45 - 2:45: Today and Monitor
"Next, the Today tab. Here are our preparation prompts and local feedback. The optional weather widget provides consented estimates. If coordinates are missing or the provider fails, local records remain usable. Measured soil results are manually entered from their source. The new setup may show a separately labelled mapped soil class; GPS does not measure NPK."

## 2:45 - 3:45: Records & Season Review
"Under Records, we navigate to the Season review for our demo soybean cycle. The synthetic totals are ₹1,800 in costs and ₹2,800 in receipts. We harvested 100 kg and sold 70 kg. The ₹1,000 difference is simply receipts minus recorded outlay, not profit. Without measured areas, per-area yield cannot be calculated."

## 3:45 - 4:30: Scan
"Let's move to Scan. We'll upload a baseline-crop image. Before processing, explicit consent is gathered. The local API returns a confidence score—this is an estimate, not a professional diagnosis. If the image is unsupported, no fabricated result is shown."

## 4:30 - 5:00: My Farm and Offline
"Finally, My Farm. Here you can see our offline-ready status and backup checks. A validated farm-record backup can be prepared here; default exports omit precise coordinates, and actual saved-file delivery must be checked on this browser. This summarizes our privacy flow, review gates, and offline capabilities."
