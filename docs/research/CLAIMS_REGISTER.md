# Claims register

Task 2 — Literature review (AgriRakshak)
Contributor: Yashi
Branch: `docs/literature-review`

Columns: claim ID | proposed claim | source | evidence strength | limitations |
recommended wording

Source numbers match [BIBLIOGRAPHY.md](BIBLIOGRAPHY.md). "Reported" marks claims that
state a source's findings; "Interpretation" marks conclusions drawn by the project
team. Evidence-strength labels:

- **Strong (reported)** — peer-reviewed/official, directly verified in the source.
- **Moderate (reported)** — verified with access caveats (mirror/Wayback/abstract) or
  scope limits.
- **Interpretation** — our conclusion from the evidence; not a source's claim.
- **Weak / TBD** — insufficient or missing evidence; needs softer wording or future
  validation.

## Claims requiring softer wording or stronger evidence

- **C-14 (calibration):** must remain **TBD**; no listed source demonstrated a
  calibration result, and no project evidence exists yet.
- **C-19 (our model accuracy):** **TBD**; do not put any accuracy value in the report
  or slides before measurement.
- **C-05 (PlantDoc "31%"):** do not quote the figure; the source's abstract/body
  wording is inconsistent and unresolved.
- **C-09 / C-20 (MCC):** keep the binary scope explicit; do not claim MCC superiority
  for our multiclass problem.
- **C-11 (GAN augmentation gains):** always labelled experiment-specific; never
  presented as expected project improvement.
- **C-15 / C-16 (ethics/responsible AI):** abstract-level only; do not attribute
  detailed conclusions or endorsements to these papers.
- **C-02 (dataset size):** state both verified figures or an approximate "about
  54,000" rather than picking one silently.
- **C-06 (93% cassava):** crop-specific supporting evidence only; do not generalize.
- **C-01 (99.35%):** must always travel with the external 31.40%/31.69% figures.
